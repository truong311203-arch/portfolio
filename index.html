RAG · flagship

GPTResident — Grounded Multi-Tenant RAG

An end-to-end Vietnamese RAG assistant for residential management. Answers come only from approved policies, with traceable citations, role/zone isolation and fail-closed LangGraph routing.

  • My part: evaluation, retrieval and indexing stack, agent-flow tuning; I also supported agent and frontend/backend refactoring.
  • Structure-aware PDF parsing and chunking; scoped hybrid pgvector + PostgreSQL FTS retrieval with weighted RRF and HyDE retries.
  • Auditable harness: 5 deterministic hard gates + isolated LLM judges over a 201-case approved benchmark (dev / validation / held-out test).
  • Results: 94.4% / 92.6% / 82.7% end-to-end pass on dev / validation / test (test includes 19 adversarial cases). On test: 100% groundedness, 89.1% answer correctness, 92.9% retrieval correctness (Recall@9 0.955), 7/7 correct refusals. 100% citation integrity and zero cross-scope leakage.

Research · MIWAI 2025

FA-GPNet — Motor-Imagery EEG Classification

Co-authored paper in the MIWAI 2025 proceedings (Springer LNAI): a hybrid brain–computer-interface pipeline that gives probabilistic predictions rather than hard labels.

  • Combines FBCSP feature extraction, a compact autoencoder and a Gaussian Process Classifier for probabilistic inference of the final output.
  • My part: the literature review of motor-imagery classification, a baseline FBCSP + Gaussian-process pipeline, and reviewing the paper.
  • My bachelor's thesis (97.5/100) was also on motor-imagery classification from EEG signals.

Agents & RAG

Multi-Agent RAG Customer Support

A LangGraph state machine that routes support queries, retrieves grounded context, drafts a reply, and has a QA agent verify it before anything is sent.

  • Intent routing, multi-turn query rewriting, QA retry loop, and interrupt()-based human review with checkpointed resume.
  • Dense + BM25 retrieval fused with RRF, then Cohere Rerank v3; hierarchical auto-merging chunks over ChromaDB.
  • FastAPI service (/chat, /chat/resume, /ingest), Streamlit UI, Docker Compose, RAGAS + LangSmith evaluation, 9 pytest modules.
